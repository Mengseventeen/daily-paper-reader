## 摘要
本研究构建了一个基于智能体的金融市场模型，通过局部羊群效应与延迟信息扩散的联合作用，解释股价的动量与反转现象。投资者对下一期价格形成异质的高斯信念，在买入、卖出和不行动之间做出选择，并根据邻近投资者的行为调整其行动概率。局部交互结构由冯·诺依曼和摩尔晶格表示，随后替换为Erdős–Rényi和Watts–Strogatz网络以增强稳健性。一个独立的信息过程通过有限速度扩散机制更新投资者信念，从而将信息调整与行为模仿区分开来。模拟结果表明，更强的羊群效应会产生空间聚集的交易行为、更大的价格波动以及更显著的超额峰度。更快的信息扩散缩短了价格趋近信号隐含价值所需的时间，而信息扩散与社会强化的结合则导致过度反应及随后的反转。对中国A股市场的实证应用将传统的CSAD和LSV指标与经Johnson SU变换后得到的滚动尾部羊群指标进行了比较。这些指标展现出相似的时变特征，并在重大市场动荡期间上升。这些发现表明信息延迟、局部社会强化以及羊群效应的最终衰减是动量与反转背后的互补机制。

## Abstract
This study develops an agent-based financial market model to explain stock-price momentum and reversal through the joint effects of local herding and delayed information diffusion. Investors form heterogeneous Gaussian beliefs about the next-period price, choose among buying, selling, and remaining inactive, and revise their action probabilities in response to neighboring investors. The local interaction structure is represented by von Neumann and Moore lattices and is later replaced by Erdős--Rényi and Watts--Strogatz networks for robustness. A separate information process updates investor beliefs through a finite-speed diffusion mechanism, allowing informational adjustment to be distinguished from behavioral imitation. The simulations show that stronger herding produces spatially clustered trading, larger price fluctuations, and more pronounced excess kurtosis in returns. Faster information diffusion reduces the time required for prices to approach the signal-implied value, whereas the combination of information diffusion and social reinforcement generates overshooting and subsequent reversal. An empirical application to China's A-share market compares conventional CSAD and LSV measures with a rolling tail-based herding indicator obtained after Johnson $S_U$ transformation. The indicators display similar time variation and rise during major market disruptions. These findings identify information delay, local social reinforcement, and the eventual decay of herding as complementary mechanisms behind momentum and reversal.
