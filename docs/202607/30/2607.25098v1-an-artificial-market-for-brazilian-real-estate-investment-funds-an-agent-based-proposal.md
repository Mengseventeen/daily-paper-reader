## 摘要
本文介绍了利用智能体建模方法开发和验证巴西房地产投资信托基金（REITs）（即Fundos de Investimento Imobiliario，FIIs）的人工市场。本工作的核心贡献在于，在单个多智能体系统中整合了FII价值链，从受空置率和运营成本影响的房地产收入生成，到股息分配，再到由订单簿双重拍卖机制中介的异质性投资者的股票交易。该模型纳入了内生宏观经济变量，如巴西基准利率Selic和通货膨胀，并通过将行为分解为基础分析者、投机者和噪声交易者成分，并根据个体金融素养水平进行调节，来表征智能体的异质性。模型使用模拟矩方法对2021年至2025年间IFIX指数（巴西REIT市场指数）的历史序列进行校准。通过两种不同方法获得的验证结果表明，模型再现了实际市场中观察到的主要典型特征：（i）校准矩的覆盖率超过75%；（ii）根据最近邻标准，96%的模拟轨迹与真实IFIX期间在结构上无法区分；（iii）绝对收益的自相关的幂律和聚集高斯性等典型特征自发出现，而未纳入校准目标函数。验证过程的结果表明，人工市场捕捉了FII市场的结构动态，为其作为分析监管政策和定价机制的计算实验室打开了前景。

## Abstract
This article presents the development and validation of an artificial market for Brazilian Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), known as Fundos de Investimento Imobiliario (FIIs), using agent-based modeling methodology. The central contribution of this work is the integration, within a single multi-agent system, of the FII value chain, from the generation of real estate revenues subject to vacancy and operational costs, through dividend distribution, to the trading of shares by heterogeneous investors mediated by a double auction mechanism with an order book. The model incorporates endogenous macroeconomic variables, such as the Selic, the Brazilian benchmark interest rate, and inflation, and represents agent heterogeneity through a behavioral decomposition into fundamentalist, speculator, and noise trader components, modulated by individual financial literacy levels. The model was calibrated using the Method of Simulated Moments applied to the historical series of the IFIX index, the Brazilian REIT market index, between 2021 and 2025. The validation results, obtained using two distinct methods, demonstrate that the model reproduces the main stylized facts observed in the real market: (i) the coverage rate of calibrated moments exceeds 75 percent; (ii) 96 percent of simulated trajectories are structurally indistinguishable from real IFIX periods according to the nearest-neighbor criterion; and (iii) stylized facts such as the power law of autocorrelations of absolute returns and aggregational Gaussianity emerge spontaneously, without being incorporated into the calibration objective function. The results of the validation process indicate that the artificial market captures structural dynamics of the FII market, opening perspectives for its use as a computational laboratory for the analysis of regulatory policies and pricing mechanisms.
