## 摘要
基于人工智能的解决方案在包括信用评分在内的许多应用中提供了提高效率的新可能性。然而，所使用的机器学习模型日益复杂，引发了对其诸多方面的担忧，可解释性也不例外。我们审视了相关的欧盟法律背景，并将这一审视与技术科学的见解相结合，以便根据技术可能性来解释相关法律条款。我们拒绝了对解释权的狭义解释，并提出了广义解释，该解释不仅涵盖技术性解释，还包括法律上的正当性论证，唯有如此才能以可操作的方式保障债权人的权利。

## Abstract
Artificial intelligence-based solutions offer new efficiency-increasing possibilities in many applications, including credit scoring. Yet, the increasing sophistication of machine-learning models in use raises concerns regarding many of their aspects, explainability notwithstanding. We review the relevant EU legal background and integrate this review with insights from technical sciences to interpret relevant legal provisions in the light of technological possibilities. We reject the narrow interpretations of the right to explanation and suggest the broad one, which encompasses not only a technical explanations but also a legal justification as the only one that allows to safeguard the creditors rights in an operative manner.
