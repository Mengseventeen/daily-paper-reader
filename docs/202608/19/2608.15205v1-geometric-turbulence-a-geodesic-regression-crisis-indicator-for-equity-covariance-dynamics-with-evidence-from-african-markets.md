## 摘要
一篮子资产的协方差矩阵是一个对称正定对象，它在弯曲流形上演化，而非平坦向量空间。对其向量化条目进行标准线性回归忽略了该几何结构，在市场压力期间，可能返回未能保持正定性的拟合结果。我们研究了在log-Euclidean和仿射不变度量下对称正定锥$\SPD(n)$上的测地线回归，以滚动样本协方差作为数据。拟合测地线的速度范数被提议为一种几何感知的湍流指标。在四个股票篮子的日度数据上——一个由十只股票组成的历史S&P子篮子（2006-2024）、一个当前的S&P 100超大盘篮子、约翰内斯堡证券交易所（JSE）的前十大上市股票以及埃及交易所（EGX）交易最活跃的十只股票——该指标在已知压力事件（2008年全球金融危机、2020年COVID崩盘、2022年美联储加息周期和2019年10月至11月埃及政治经济紧张事件）中清晰达到峰值，无需任何校准。在崩盘窗口的保留评估中，log-Euclidean回归的测地线均方误差比欧几里得普通最小二乘法小150至300倍，因为后者产生的非SPD预测在本征度量下会发散。一个朴素的基于速度的降风险策略将最大回撤降低了2.5个百分点，以年度回报下降1.4个百分点为代价，这与一个缓慢移动的指标一致，该指标能可靠地发出压力信号，但在转化为战术交易方面并不完美。所有数据、代码和实验均可从配套仓库中复现。

## Abstract
The covariance matrix of a basket of assets is a symmetric positive-definite object that evolves on a curved manifold, not on a flat vector space. Standard linear regression on its vectorised entries ignores that geometry and, in periods of market stress, can return fits that fail to be positive definite. We study geodesic regression on the symmetric positive-definite cone $\SPD(n)$ under the log-Euclidean and affine-invariant metrics, with the rolling sample covariance of a basket of log-returns as data. The fitted geodesic's velocity norm is proposed as a geometry-aware turbulence indicator. On daily data for four equity baskets- a ten-stock historical S\&P sub-basket (2006-2024), a current S\&P~100 mega-cap basket, the top ten listings of the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE), and the ten most-traded Egyptian Exchange (EGX) tickers the indicator peaks cleanly on known stress episodes (the 2008 global financial crisis, the 2020 COVID crash, the 2022 Fed rate-hike cycle, and the October-November 2019 Egyptian political-economic tension episode) without any calibration. On hold-out evaluation in crash windows, log-Euclidean regression achieves 150 to 300 times smaller geodesic mean squared error than Euclidean ordinary least squares, because the latter produces non-SPD forecasts that blow up under the intrinsic metric. A naive velocity-aware de-risking strategy reduces maximum drawdown by 2.5 percentage points at the cost of 1.4 percentage points of annual return, consistent with a slow-moving indicator that signals stress reliably but translates imperfectly into tactical trading. All data, code, and experiments are reproducible from the companion repository.
