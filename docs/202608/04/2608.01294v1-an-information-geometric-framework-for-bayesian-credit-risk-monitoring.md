## 摘要
我们提出了一种用于信用风险监测的信息几何框架，其中银行对借款人的认知通过潜在信用状况和财务脆弱性维度上的后验分布来表示。在线性-高斯设定下，贝叶斯更新将观察到的行为评分映射为高斯后验信念，这些信念构成一个带有Fisher信息度量的统计流形。在公共协方差情形下，诱导几何退化为后验均值上的Mahalanobis度量，而借款人特定的协方差矩阵允许信息距离同时考虑预期风险和评估不确定性。模拟实验说明了如何使用Kullback-Leibler散度和Jeffreys散度来比较投资组合分段。该框架为信用监测提供了一种几何解释，即借款人风险后验信念的演化。

## Abstract
We propose an information-geometric framework for credit risk monitoring in which a bank's knowledge of a borrower is represented by a posterior distribution over latent dimensions of creditworthiness and financial fragility.   Under a linear-Gaussian specification, Bayesian updating maps observed behavioural scores into Gaussian posterior beliefs, which form a statistical manifold endowed with the Fisher information metric.   In the common-covariance case, the induced geometry reduces to the Mahalanobis metric on posterior means, while borrower-specific covariance matrices allow informational distances to account for both expected risk and assessment uncertainty.   Simulation exercises illustrate how Kullback-Leibler and Jeffreys divergences can be used to compare portfolio segments.   The framework provides a geometric interpretation of credit monitoring as the evolution of posterior beliefs over borrower risk.
